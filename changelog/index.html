Engineering log

What changed on this site, when, and what it did to speed, security or reliability. Mistakes are logged like everything else. This page is in English; subscribe by feed.

6 November 2026

Performance panel: the budget now compares like with like

The panel showed 13 requests next to a limit of 12. The extra requests were the icon, the web manifest, the prefetch rules and Cloudflare’s own script: things that depend on the browser and the host, not on the page. They are now listed on their own line with their size, and the budget is compared with the page itself. A test keeps the pattern that separates them in sync with the shipped script.

6 November 2026

Layout fixes from a visual pass on phones, tablets and laptops

The navigation was cut off on tablets (the links and the search button ran past the screen between 768 and about 1100 pixels), and the floating badges around the portrait were cut off on 1280-pixel laptops. The links now show from 1280 pixels and a menu below that; the badges keep clear of the screen edge and of the text. On phones the years badge no longer hangs off the screen, the profile highlights stack one per row so long words such as Ciberseguridad fit, the scroll cue is a 44-pixel target, and the Italian footer line is in Italian. Ten layout tests across the three languages fail on the old layout.

6 November 2026

Engineering log, build notes and keyboard shortcuts

This log, a page on how the site is built (decisions and what each one costs), and keyboard shortcuts: ? lists them, l switches language, g then a letter jumps to a section.

6 November 2026

Contact card link fixed on the Spanish and Italian pages

The Save to contacts link of the release below pointed at /es/ and /it/ paths that do not exist (404). It is root-relative now, and the test requests the link as the browser resolves it from each page. Found by checking the live site after the deploy.

6 November 2026

Instant navigation, a live performance panel, trust pages, contact card

Chromium prerenders the next page when you rest on a link; analytics and the consent banner wait until the page is actually shown. Early Hints send the critical file list before the page is ready. A footer panel shows what your own visit cost, from the browser’s Performance API. Trust pages in three languages state how the site protects visitors and ships changes. A vCard per language, and a clean printed page.

6 November 2026

Page transitions made reliable

The cross-fade between languages sometimes did not run. Cause: the opt-in rule sat inside a reduced-motion media query, which Chromium can evaluate too late on the new page. It is now one top-level rule, and reduced motion switches the animation off instead. Failures at the top of the page under heavy load fell from about 58% to about 12%; the rest is Chromium skipping transitions on a saturated machine, which gives an ordinary page load.

6 November 2026

Test suite 4 minutes to about 77 seconds

The suite runs as parallel groups behind one required check, and the CI browser is installed without the system package manager, which had stalled one run for 8 minutes. Production deploys run the same groups.

6 November 2026

Whole head visible in the About portrait

On desktop the photo was cropped at the forehead; it is now aligned to the top.

5 November 2026

Portrait no longer flashes square when switching language

The transition snapshot was taken of the image inside the round frame. The name now sits on the circle itself, and a test fails if anything inside it is named.

5 November 2026

Performance budget enforced in tests

A first visit may use 200 KB, 12 requests, 30 KB of script, 11 KB of CSS, 110 KB of fonts and 25 KB of images; a change over any limit fails the build. At the time of writing a visit is 9 requests and about 152 KB.

5 November 2026

Cross-fade between language pages

Switching language fades instead of flashing white, with the navigation bar and portrait held in place. Pure CSS, no library.

5 November 2026

Production health check every 30 minutes

An automatic check confirms the site and its security headers, the redirects from the old and www addresses, certificate validity (at least 14 days), mail routing and the security contact file. A failure sends an alert.

5 November 2026

Link previews in the page language

Each language page shares its own 1200 by 630 card, rendered at build time from the page content.

5 November 2026

Command menu, live Milan time, llms.txt and security.txt

Ctrl or Cmd K opens a menu to jump anywhere; the hero shows the time in Milan. /llms.txt summarises the site for AI assistants and /.well-known/security.txt gives a security contact (RFC 9116).

5 November 2026

Mobile menu readable after scrolling

The drawer was clipped by the blurred navigation bar once the page had scrolled. It now sits outside the bar.

5 November 2026

New primary domain

The site moved to carlosfernandezsanmillan.com. The previous domain and the www address redirect permanently, keeping path and query. Mail addresses on the new domain forward to the owner and cannot be used to send.

5 November 2026

Production on Cloudflare Pages

Real security headers (a strict Content Security Policy, HSTS, no framing) and one-year caching for fingerprinted files. Publishing needs a typed confirmation and a reviewer’s approval; the previous hosting stays available as a fallback.
